# Privacy notice

SecretEngerl stores nothing on servers. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the host sees

The app is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every page request for security purposes. The draw is not part of that: it sits after the `#` in the link, and browsers never send that part to a server.

## Where your data stays

What you enter into a round stays on the devices of those taking part and in the chats the links go through. On the organizer's device the round is kept in the browser until 14 days after the exchange, or until "Delete round now". The receiving side stores nothing.
