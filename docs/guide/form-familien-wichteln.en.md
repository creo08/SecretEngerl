# Draw Family Wichteln online

For families who draw every year: people who live together do not draw each other, last year's pairings do not come back, and everyone can leave a hint for their Engerl.

[Start with this template](app:?vorlage=familien-wichteln)

## How it works with SecretEngerl

1. [Open the template "Family Wichteln"](app:?vorlage=familien-wichteln).
2. Enter the names, one per line. A blank line separates groups who should not draw each other.
3. Enter last year's pairings and the hints, then tap "Draw".
4. Send every Engerl their draw as a link in a one-to-one chat.

## What is set

- People in the same group, such as one household, do not draw each other, and there are no mutual pairs.
- You can enter last year's pairings by hand; they are excluded this year.
- A hint per person, such as wishes, sizes or "no nuts", goes only to that person's Engerl. No diagnoses are needed.
