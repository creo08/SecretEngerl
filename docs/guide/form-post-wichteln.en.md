# Draw Postal Wichteln online

For groups that do not meet: everyone draws a name and sends the gift by post. Often the group unwraps together on a video call.

[Start with this template](app:?vorlage=post-wichteln)

## How it works with SecretEngerl

1. [Open the template "Postal Wichteln"](app:?vorlage=post-wichteln).
2. Enter the names, one per line. A blank line separates groups who should not draw each other.
3. Enter the exchange date, the budget and everyone's address, then tap "Draw".
4. Send every Engerl their draw as a link in a one-to-one chat.

## What is set

- Each address appears only in the draw of the Engerl who sends to that person. Nobody else sees it.
- The address is stored only on the organizer's device while the round runs.
- People in the same group do not draw each other, and there are no mutual pairs.
