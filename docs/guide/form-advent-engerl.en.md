# Draw an Advent-Engerl online

Through Advent, every Engerl secretly slips their Bengerl small joys, about once a week: a little gift, a note, a good deed. The reveal is at the party.

[Start with this template](app:?vorlage=advent-engerl)

## How it works with SecretEngerl

1. [Open the template "Advent-Engerl"](app:?vorlage=advent-engerl).
2. Enter the names, one per line. A blank line separates groups who should not draw each other.
3. Enter the day of the reveal, a small budget per week and, if you like, a theme per Advent week, then tap "Draw".
4. Send every Engerl their draw as a link in a one-to-one chat.

## What is set

- Every draw names the themes of the four Advent weeks, if set.
- "Add to calendar" creates an event for each Sunday of Advent and one for the reveal, without the Bengerl's name.
- People in the same group do not draw each other, and there are no mutual pairs.
