# Draw Engerl-Bengerl online

The classic round: everyone draws one name, gives one gift within the budget, and the gifts change hands at the party.

[Start with this template](app:?vorlage=engerl-bengerl)

## How it works with SecretEngerl

1. [Open the template "Engerl-Bengerl classic"](app:?vorlage=engerl-bengerl).
2. Enter the names, one per line. A blank line separates groups who should not draw each other.
3. Enter the exchange date and the budget, then tap "Draw".
4. Send every Engerl their draw as a link in a one-to-one chat.

## What is set

- People in the same group, such as a household or a couple, do not draw each other.
- No mutual pairs: two people never give to each other.
- A budget and the exchange date go into every draw.
- The reveal happens at the party, in person.
