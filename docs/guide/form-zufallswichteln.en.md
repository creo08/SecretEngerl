# Zufallswichteln: draw only at the party

Everyone brings a gift for a person nobody knows yet. Only at the party does one phone draw who gets whose gift, and it shows the pairs one at a time. Nobody gets a link in advance.

[Start this game](app:?spiel=zufallswichteln)

## How it works with SecretEngerl

1. [Open Zufallswichteln](app:?spiel=zufallswichteln).
2. Enter the names, one per line. A blank line separates groups who should not give to each other.
3. At the party, tap "Draw now".
4. Show the pairs one at a time; at the end all pairs are on one page.

## What is set

- Nobody gets their own gift.
- People in the same group do not give to each other, and there are no mutual pairs.
- Nothing is stored and there are no links. After a reload the draw is gone.
