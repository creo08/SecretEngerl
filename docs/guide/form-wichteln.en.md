# Draw Wichteln online

In Wichteln, everyone secretly draws a name and gives that person a gift as their Wichtel. In Austria the same custom is called Engerl-Bengerl. SecretEngerl draws without anyone seeing the assignment, and without a server.

[Start with the classic template](app:?vorlage=engerl-bengerl)

## How it works with SecretEngerl

1. [Open the template "Engerl-Bengerl classic"](app:?vorlage=engerl-bengerl).
2. Enter the names, one per line. A blank line separates groups who should not draw each other.
3. Enter the exchange date and the budget, then tap "Draw".
4. Send every Wichtel their draw as a link in a one-to-one chat.

## Matching templates

- [Engerl-Bengerl classic](app:engerl-bengerl/): one gift within the budget, exchanged at the party.
- [Schrottwichteln](app:schrottwichteln/): buy nothing, give what you already have.
- [Family Wichteln](app:familien-wichteln/): with hints and without last year's pairings.
- [Office Wichteln](app:buero-wichteln/): small budget, a theme if you like.
- [Postal Wichteln](app:post-wichteln/): gifts by post, the address only in the Engerl's draw.
