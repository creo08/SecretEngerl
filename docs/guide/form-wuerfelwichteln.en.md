# Play Würfelwichteln on one phone

The gifts lie in the middle. In turn, everyone rolls the die, and the number says who takes, swaps or passes on. When the time is up, everyone keeps what they hold. The phone rolls, shows the rule and keeps the time.

[Start this game](app:?spiel=wuerfelwichteln)

## How it works with SecretEngerl

1. [Open Würfelwichteln](app:?spiel=wuerfelwichteln).
2. Enter the names, one per line, in seating order if you do not play in random order.
3. Choose the playing time, change the dice rules if you like and tap "Start game".
4. Whoever's turn it is taps "Roll", does what the rule says and hands on with "Next".

## What is set

- A 6 takes a gift from the middle, a 1 swaps with anyone, a 2 passes all gifts clockwise, a 3 swaps with the left neighbour, a 4 with the right neighbour, and a 5 picks two people who swap. Every rule can be changed.
- The playing time is 15 minutes. At the end there is a tone and, where possible, a vibration; the screen stays on until then.
- The direction can be reversed during the game.
- A drinking rule is possible but off by default.
- Played as Schrottwichteln, everyone brings something they already own.
- There are no links. The game stays on the phone and deletes itself after 24 hours.
