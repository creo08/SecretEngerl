# How it works

SecretEngerl draws names for Engerl-Bengerl, the Austrian Secret Santa, for Wichteln and for Secret Santa. Everything happens in your browser; no server sees the names.

## 1. Pick a template

Choose how your group plays: [Engerl-Bengerl classic](app:engerl-bengerl/), [Schrottwichteln](app:schrottwichteln/), [Family Wichteln](app:familien-wichteln/), [Office Wichteln](app:buero-wichteln/), [Postal Wichteln](app:post-wichteln/) or [Advent-Engerl](app:advent-engerl/). Each one fills in sensible defaults.

## 2. Who plays

Enter the names, one per line. A blank line separates groups, such as a household, a couple, a family or a team: people in the same group never draw each other. Without any blank line, everyone plays on their own. Add the exchange date and, if your template has one, the budget. Up to 50 people can take part. If you organize several rounds, give each one a name; it is only for your own overview and never goes into a draw.

## 3. Rules, if you want them

"No mutual pairs" is on: two people never give to each other. Under "More options" you can add exclusions (siblings, a manager and their team), must rules, last year's pairings and a hint per person for their Engerl. Only write what the Engerl needs to know. No diagnoses are needed: "no nuts" is enough.

If the rules cannot all hold, the app says why, for example "Anna, Bernd and Clara may only give to two people between them".

## 4. Draw and send

Tap "Draw". The app creates the assignment on your device, with every valid draw exactly equally likely, and encrypts one link per person. You see nothing of who drew whom.

Then tap "Send" next to each name. Your phone's share sheet opens; pick the one-to-one chat with that person. Send every link privately, never to the group chat. Where sharing is not available, the link is copied and you paste it yourself.

## 5. Open

The person taps the link. The app removes the draw from the address bar at once and shows "Tap to reveal". Then it reads "You are Bernd's Engerl", with budget, date and hint. Nothing is stored; the link in the chat opens it again.

## 6. During the round

- **Send again:** if someone loses their link, open the round on your device and send it again.
- **Someone drops out:** the Engerl of that person takes over their Bengerl, and exactly one person gets a new link. If that breaks a rule, the app tells you and offers a new draw.
- **Draw again:** everyone gets a new link and the draw number goes up. Old links still open, so tell the group which draw counts.
- **Stage reveal:** at the party, "Start reveal" shows one pair at a time on your device, following the chain. Until the exchange day it sits under "During the round", needs "Ich spicke" and is logged.
- **Emergency:** "View assignment" shows one person at a time after you type "Ich spicke" or hold the button. Every view is logged.

Fourteen days after the exchange the round deletes itself from your device, and the links show "Round expired".
