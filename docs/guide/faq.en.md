# FAQ

## Do I need an account?

No. Nobody needs an account, not the organizer and not the people taking part.

## Where are the names stored?

Only on the organizer's device, so links can be sent again. Fourteen days after the exchange the round deletes itself. Nothing goes to a server.

## Can the organizer see who drew whom?

Not while drawing and sending. For real emergencies there is "View assignment": one person at a time, after typing "Ich spicke" or holding a button, and every view is logged. The device does hold the assignment, so this is fair play, not a lock.

## Why is it fair?

Every valid draw is exactly equally likely, with all your rules. Equally likely draws do not mean equally likely people: with groups, a member of another couple is drawn a little more often than a single person. That follows from the rules.

## Someone lost their link. What now?

Open the round on your device and tap "Send" next to their name again.

## Someone drops out. Do we have to draw again?

Usually not. Their Engerl takes over their Bengerl, and only that one person gets a new link. If that breaks a rule, the app says so and offers a new draw.

## How do we avoid last year's pairs?

In the round, "Remember for next year" saves a file with names, groups, pairings, budget and hints. Next year, open it with "Open file" on the start page: the app fills in everything and excludes last year's pairings. On the same device the app also keeps the ended round for you under "From last year". Without a password, anyone with the file can read who gave to whom and the hints, so give it one if it travels.

## Can I send the links in the group chat?

Please do not. Each link holds one person's draw. Send it in a one-to-one chat.

## Does it work offline?

Yes, once the app has been opened. On iPhone and in Safari, add it to the home screen so your round is not deleted after seven days without a visit.

## What does the link preview show?

The same thing for every draw: the app's name. The draw itself is behind the `#` and not part of the preview.
