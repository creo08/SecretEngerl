# Draw Office Wichteln online

For teams at work: a small budget, a theme if you like, and the organizer plays along.

[Start with this template](app:?vorlage=buero-wichteln)

## How it works with SecretEngerl

1. [Open the template "Office Wichteln"](app:?vorlage=buero-wichteln).
2. Enter the names, one per line. A blank line separates groups who should not draw each other.
3. Enter the exchange date, the budget and a theme if you like, then tap "Draw".
4. Send every Engerl their draw as a link in a one-to-one chat.

## What is set

- A budget of 10 to start with, which you can change.
- A theme is optional, for example "local" or "something to eat".
- Exclusions under "More options", for example a manager and their team.
- No mutual pairs.
