# Office Wichteln

For teams at work: a small budget, a theme if you like, and the organizer plays along.

[Start with this template](app:?vorlage=buero-wichteln)

## What is set

- A budget of 10 € to start with, which you can change.
- A theme is optional, for example "local" or "something to eat".
- Exclusions under "More options", for example a manager and their team.
- No mutual pairs.
