# Play Räuberwichteln on one phone

Everyone brings one wrapped gift. In turn, a player unwraps a new gift or steals one that is already unwrapped. Whoever is robbed goes again at once. One phone at the table keeps track: whose turn it is, who holds what and how often a gift has been stolen.

[Start this game](app:?spiel=raeuberwichteln)

## How it works with SecretEngerl

1. [Open Räuberwichteln](app:?spiel=raeuberwichteln).
2. Enter the names, one per line, and choose "Random" or "As typed" for the order.
3. Tap "Start game" and put the phone in the middle.
4. Whoever's turn it is unwraps a new gift or taps "Steal" next to an unwrapped one.

## What is set

- A gift is locked after 3 steals; the number can be changed.
- Whoever is robbed may not steal back the gift they just lost.
- As an option, the first player may swap once more at the end.
- There are no links. The game stays on the phone, so a reload does not end it, and deletes itself after 24 hours.

## White Elephant (English)

> Everyone brings one wrapped gift. In turn, players either open a new gift or steal one that has already been opened.
